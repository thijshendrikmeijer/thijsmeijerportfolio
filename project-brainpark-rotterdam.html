Welstandsparagraaf Brainpark Rotterdam

An image quality plan that guards unity while leaving room for architectural expression.

My roleVisualising the guidelines
ClientMunicipality of Rotterdam
EmployerGROUP A
TypeProfessional project
WhenMar 2022 – Sep 2023
LocationRotterdam
CategoriesUrban design, Urban planning

Overview

Brainpark I, a 1980s office district between the Erasmus University campus and the A16, is transforming into a mixed area where living, working and leisure meet. Because of its special character, the municipality designated it a development area in the Rotterdamse Welstandsnota and asked GROUP A to write the Welstandsparagraaf: the criteria the welstand committee uses to assess every new design in the district.

The document translates the urban plan into practical guidelines for architects, from building volumes, plinths and entrances to courtyards, roofs, greenery and materials. The recurring question: how do you keep unity at the urban scale without choking the architectural expression of a single building?

My role

I was responsible for visualising the rules: making clear on paper what each guideline means spatially for the area.

  • Translated the urban plan’s guidelines into a series of explanatory diagrams.
  • Sparred with the project leader on how to depict the rules for architecture. The diagram series on this page grew out of those discussions.

Result

  • The Welstandsparagraaf was adopted by the Municipality of Rotterdam.
  • The client was pleased with how simple and effective the document and its descriptions are.

Worked with: project leader at GROUP A; the Municipality of Rotterdam.

Tools: SketchUp, Illustrator, InDesign, AutoCAD
