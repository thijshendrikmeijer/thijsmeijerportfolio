Manifesto

“Play is the highest form of research,” Albert Einstein said — and every project of mine starts as an experiment, made within the limits of the earth.

Design that begins with curiosity rather than certainty. I model, sketch, map and test. Because the most resilient places are the ones that were allowed to be playful first.

Constraints are the rules of the game: budgets, climate, mobility and people. Within them I look for the move nobody expected, always staying within what the planet can carry.
